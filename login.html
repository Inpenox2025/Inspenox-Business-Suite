Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to pay extra for sending messages? ▸

Your plan fee covers the platform — sending offers, managing customers, inbox, analytics, etc. For messages actually delivered via WhatsApp, you only pay WhatsApp's official rate (₹0.86 for promotions, ₹0.12 for order updates). We don't add any extra fee on top. Most other platforms charge ₹1.00–₹1.25 per message.

Can I change or cancel my plan anytime? ▸

Yes! You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from your dashboard. There are no long-term contracts or cancellation fees. If you cancel, you'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

I'm not tech-savvy. Is it hard to set up? ▸

Not at all! We guide you through every step — from connecting your WhatsApp number to sending your first campaign. Most businesses get started in under 15 minutes. And if you get stuck, our support team is here to help you over chat or call.

Will my WhatsApp number get banned? ▸

No. We use the Official WhatsApp Business API (approved by Meta/Facebook). This is completely different from unofficial bulk messaging tools that risk bans. Your messages are sent through the same system that big brands like Flipkart, Swiggy, and Zomato use.

What kind of messages can I send? ▸

You can send promotional offers & discounts, new product announcements, festival greetings, order confirmations, delivery updates, payment reminders, and much more — all with images, videos, buttons, and links to make them interactive and engaging.

Can my team members also use the dashboard? ▸

Yes! The Growth plan includes 5 team members who can all log in and reply to customers, send campaigns, and manage contacts. The Business Pro plan includes unlimited team members. It's like giving your whole team a shared WhatsApp for business.
