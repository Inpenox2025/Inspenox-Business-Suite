Privacy Policy

Compliance & data protection policy for Inspenox Business Suite For Marketing messaging platform.

1. Application & Ownership

The Inspenox Business Suite For Marketing platform is operated for business communications and omni-channel customer engagement by Inspenox.

2. Information We Collect

We collect customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, opt-in consent records, and messaging logs required to deliver business updates, promotional offers, and customer service communications.

3. Customer Opt-In & Marketing Consent

In compliance with messaging policies and data protection regulations, all contacts agree: "I agree to receive WhatsApp, Email, and SMS promotional updates & service announcements from Inspenox Business Suite For Marketing." Explicit consent is collected during contact submission and bulk import.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your customer directory and message logs are securely stored in encrypted database infrastructure with strict access controls.

5. Data Sharing & Protection

Data is managed strictly for legitimate business messaging via Inspenox Business Suite For Marketing. We do not sell or rent contact data to unauthorized third parties.

6. Customer Opt-Out Rights

Customers can opt out of receiving messages at any time by replying "STOP" or requesting contact deletion from our directory.

Terms of Service

Platform terms, messaging guidelines, and acceptable use policy for Inspenox Business Suite.

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or using the Inspenox Business Suite platform, you agree to these Terms of Service and applicable messaging policies.

2. Authorized Messaging & Scope

Communications sent via this platform are authorized for legitimate business operations. Messages include order updates, customer service replies, marketing announcements, and promotional offers.

3. Acceptable Use & Anti-Spam Policy

Unsolicited spamming, bulk un-consented messaging, and sending unauthorized content are strictly prohibited. All contacts must have explicitly consented to receive updates.

4. Quality Rating & Account Standing

Users are responsible for maintaining high messaging quality standards. Failing to preserve opt-in records may impact channel account standings.

5. Ownership & Maintenance

The software platform, database schema, and branding rights are maintained by Inspenox.
